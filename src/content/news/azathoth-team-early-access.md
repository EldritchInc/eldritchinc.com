azathoth-team is the commercial team version of Azathoth's shared server, built on the same open core. The early access list is open.

## Built for the questions leaders ask

Whoever owns AI at a company gets asked five things: what it costs, whether it works, whether anyone uses it, what could go wrong, and whether it pays off. azathoth-team is built to answer them.

## What's planned

- **Sign-in through your identity provider,** with OIDC or SAML, so every caller is a person.
- **Roles per remote.** The same person can hold different roles on staging and production. Approving models and promoting to production are role-gated.
- **A searchable, exportable audit** of every push, pull, run, promotion, approval and restricted read.
- **Rules for restricted data:** who may read, run or add it, with an assurance report.
- **Organization reporting across remotes:** rollups, cost centres and chargeback, budgets, forecasts and alerts, value and ROI, and scheduled, exported reports.

## The order of work

Azathoth 1.5 brings the editor to full parity with the command line. 1.6 turns a remote into an Azathoth you work on, with every call a named, checkable action. azathoth-team builds on that.

## Pricing, and what stays free

azathoth-team will be paid, per seat, with founding-team pricing for early teams. The engine, the MCP server and the single-user editor stay MIT licensed and free.
