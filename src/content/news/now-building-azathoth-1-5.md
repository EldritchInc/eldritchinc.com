One principle: everything the command line can do, the editor can do. That's Azathoth 1.5, and we're building it now. Here's what it brings to the editor, in order.

## 1. Production

Promote, roll back, see what's in production, and invoke it. A Production tab lists every invocation, and each one has its own page: input, result, the reason for any failure, and resume.

## 2. Sensitivity and model policy

Label inputs public, internal or restricted, and see those labels everywhere. You're warned before restricted data is shown. Declare a model's no-retention and no-training policy, with who declared it and when.

## 3. Judges, feedback and benchmarks

Add judges. Mark runs good or bad, with a reason and a corrected output. Build benchmarks, and turn real runs into cases, production runs included. A case is never added without a person choosing its expected output, and it's never less sensitive than the run it came from.

## 4. Optimize

Run optimization, inspect experiments, compare benchmarks, and promote the winner. Then see what it saved: cost per run before and after, at the same score.

## 5. Spend and reporting

Spend per workflow and per model, by day, week and month, with estimated and billed costs shown apart. Plus quality, reliability, adoption and change history.
